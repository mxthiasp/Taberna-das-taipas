From Taipas, for Taipas

The Taberna opened in October 2015 (to be confirmed), started by Miguel Pereira (to be confirmed) after more than twenty years (to be confirmed) in hotels and restaurants.

The town had good restaurants serving full plates, but nowhere relaxed for small plates to share in the middle of the table. The name is a tribute to Caldas das Taipas.

The recipes bring together Minho and Iberian tradition (game alheira with turnip greens, broken eggs, queen scallops from the griddle) with ideas of our own, such as the brie fondue with forest berries and the whisky-flambéed prawns. And the francesinha sauce, of course.
